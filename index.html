01 — About

A cybersecurity major learning to think like the threat.

I'm currently studying cybersecurity, focused on the space between offense and defense — how systems fail, and how they're built to fail safely instead. Most of my work lives in labs, CTFs, and personal projects where I can break things on purpose before someone else does it by accident.

I'm drawn to the quieter side of security: log analysis, network behavior, and the long trail of evidence most incidents leave behind. My goal is a career on a blue team, hardening infrastructure and catching what shouldn't be there.
